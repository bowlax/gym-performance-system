What it does

GymPerformance gives every WOLF member a personal record of their training. Your personal bests, your sessions, your progression — one account you can open on your phone or in a browser.

It's built around the exercises you already do at the gym. Every PB exercise on the board is tracked automatically — log a session, and it tells you if you've set a new personal best.

How to get started

Pick iPhone or web above, then the flow is the same: connect with TeamUp, and your training history is there.

  1. Open GymPerformance On iPhone: install via TestFlight (free TestFlight app, then this invite link). On the web: open the web app in your browser.
  2. Connect with TeamUp Sign in with the same TeamUp membership you use at the gym. That links your account so your training data backs up and follows you between iPhone and web.
  3. See your board If you already have history with us, it appears automatically. If you're starting fresh, add your current personal bests so the board has somewhere to begin — you can update them later. First-run PB setup is guided on iPhone; on the web you can add manual PBs from an exercise's progression screen.
  4. Log sessions and watch progression After training, log what you did. The board updates, and each exercise has a progression chart so you can see how your PBs move over time.

Frequently asked questions

Is it the same account on iPhone and web?

Yes. Connect with the same TeamUp login on either surface — it's one account, not two copies of your data. Your board, sessions, and progression are the same wherever you open GymPerformance.

Is my data private?

On iPhone, until you connect with TeamUp your training data stays on your device. Once you connect (on iPhone or web), it backs up so it can follow you across surfaces and your coaches can support you. Read the full privacy policy for details.

Can I disconnect my account?

Yes — on the iPhone app, go to Settings and tap Disconnect. That stops sync on that device; your history stays in our system and on the phone, so reconnecting later picks up where you left off. Disconnect isn't available on the web; use the iPhone app if you need to disconnect.

What iPhone do I need?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later. That covers iPhone XS and newer from 2018 onwards. The web app works in a modern browser on phone or desktop.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free for Wolf Way of Life Fitness members.

What is TestFlight?

TestFlight is Apple's official platform for distributing apps before they're on the App Store. It's safe, free, and made by Apple. The iPhone app will move to the App Store in future. You don't need TestFlight for the web app.

Will I lose my data if the app updates?

No. Your sessions, personal bests, and history are preserved when the iPhone app updates through TestFlight, and the same connected history is what you see on the web.

Which exercises are tracked?

All the PB exercises on the gym board - including squats, deadlifts, bench press, overhead press, dumbbell exercises, chin-ups, cable row, plank, ski 500m, and bike. Conditioning exercises will be added in a future update.
